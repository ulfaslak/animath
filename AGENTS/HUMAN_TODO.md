# Human TODO

Tasks that genuinely require human action — accounts, consents, decisions that need human judgment. Running servers, migrations, and database operations are NOT human tasks; agents do those.

Each item says what the game does today and asks only what a person must do, in plain words: a step an agent can take stays out of it, even beside one only the human can take.

The human may answer inline. Agents act on the answers from the working tree and commit only the finished edit, never the notes; an answer still being acted on keeps a one-line note, with its lane, until the lane merges.
