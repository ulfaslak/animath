# Human TODO

Tasks that genuinely require human action — accounts, consents, decisions that need human judgment. Running servers, migrations, and database operations are NOT human tasks; agents do those.

Each item says what the game does today and asks only what a person must do, in plain words: a step an agent can take stays out of it, even beside one only the human can take.

The human may answer inline. Agents act on the answers from the working tree and commit only the finished edit, never the notes; an answer still being acted on keeps a one-line note, with its lane, until the lane merges.

One list, unnumbered because items are cleared in any order: cite an item by its title, never by a number, and a proposed CLAUDE.md edit by its letter, which it keeps until the human settles it. Product questions come first, then checks only a person can make, then guardrail edits waiting for approval.

- **Should a friend's animals in a friendly match go in the animal book?** Today a kind goes in the book as seen only when a wild battle with it starts; a friendly match adds nothing, because a match changes nothing in a game but the puzzles solved ([[DECISIONS]] § Multiplayer, [[INVARIANTS]] § Match). The animal book's brief asked that an animal a kid faces in a match count as seen. Say yes or no. Yes changes that decision and that invariant: a match would also put the other kid's animals that come out in front into this kid's book as seen, never as caught (the other page's team is its own word, so a changed page could show a kid kinds they then have seen).
- **Try a real tablet and two real windows.** Touch was only tested in headless Chrome pretending to be a tablet, never on an iPad in Safari, and two windows of the game only as headless pages, never side by side on a screen with the behind card. On the tablet, also name an animal twelve W's (pause menu, New name) and visit the doctor: in Chrome that list fits with 8 px to spare (PR #49), and Safari's Nunito may measure a little wider. And walk with a thumb held on the D-pad, then switch to another app and come back: the arrow should be out and nothing walking until the thumb presses again (PR #63 checked this only with pretend `blur` and `visibilitychange` events). And hold a card of the party column still, then drag it (a quick swipe should scroll the column instead): the hold-then-drag was only tried with CDP's pretend fingers.
